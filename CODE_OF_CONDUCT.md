# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors, maintainers and users are expected to be respectful, technically honest and constructive.

Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or deliberately mislead other contributors.

## Security discussions

Do not publish exploitable vulnerability details in ordinary issues. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this standard.

The goal is a community where difficult technical problems can be discussed directly without making the project hostile to people who report them.
